## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, highly trustworthy, yet deeply encouraging environment tailored for Indonesian junior high school (SMP) educators navigating the Kurikulum Merdeka framework. Teaching staff balance heavy administrative burdens, lesson planning (Modul Ajar), and formative assessment drafting. The UI serves as an empowering digital copilot: institutional and respectful of national academic standards, but warm, modern, and friction-free.

The design movement combines **Modern Corporate Clarity** with **Curated Editorial Functionality**:
- **Structured Authority:** Anchored by structured grid systems, balanced line work, and institutional navy tones that reassure educators regarding curriculum compliance and standard alignment (Capaian Pembelajaran / Alur Tujuan Pembelajaran).
- **Productivity & Empathy:** Generous white space, clear contrast levels, and low cognitive load ensure ease of use across long lesson-planning sessions on laptops or school computer lab monitors.
- **Approachable Modernity:** Subtly rounded surfaces, balanced typography, and deliberate accent cues remove cold bureaucracy while preserving professional competence.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional integrity with active workspace feedback:

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A`) — Deep Academic Navy:** Anchor for navigation sidebars, high-level headers, active tabs, and primary administrative prompts. Conveys institutional trust and curriculum rigor.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`) — Scholarly Cobalt:** The interactive engine. Powers primary action buttons, focused input borders, generated content highlights, and link states.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`) — Emerald Growth:** Reserved for success states, completed Capaian Pembelajaran (CP) indicators, approved lesson plans, and verified module exports.
- **Accent Warning / In-Progress (`#F59E0B`) — Warm Amber:** Flags draft states, pending revisions, differentiated learning tags, and assessment rubric alerts.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas:** Layered slate foundations (`#F8FAFC` for base canvas, `#FFFFFF` for elevated modular cards, and `#F1F5F9` for secondary work surfaces, toolbars, and inactive panels).
- **Text & Borders:** Crisp dark slate (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast primary typography, muted slate (`#475569`) for instructional hints, and precise 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`) providing tactile structural division.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural elements, headers, and UI labels with **Inter** for dense instructional narrative and generated material reading. 

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** introduces friendly Indonesian-developed geometric balance, giving administrative labels, subject headers (e.g., *Ilmu Pengetahuan Alam - Fase D*), and generator wizard steps a modern, human warmth without losing institutional authority.
- **Inter** ensures superior legibility for longer educational bodies of text, generated rubrics, lesson objective paragraphs, and downloadable worksheets, maintaining high readability on legacy screens common in public school infrastructures.
- Keep letter spacing slightly condensed on large display headings to preserve tightness and authoritative impact, while keeping label tokens spaced outwards for scanning efficiency.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible 12-column responsive grid paired with dedicated workspace side-panels.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Fixed navigation sidebar (260px), accompanied by a 12-column fluid grid canvas with `2rem` screen margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Generator views adopt a dual-pane workspace: a 5-column parameters and input panel beside a 7-column real-time document preview canvas.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px - 1279px):** Collapsible icon rail navigation (72px), 8-column content layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Document preview transitions into a stacked configuration or tabbed preview mode.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with `1rem` margins and bottom app-bar navigation, focusing on reviewing, sharing, or quick edits of generated modules.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** All interactive component paddings follow the 4px/8px rhythm. Form field blocks and card interiors prioritize spacious internal gaps (`space-lg`) to prevent teacher visual fatigue during complex curriculum configuration.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **crisp 1px boundary lines** and **soft ambient shadows**, avoiding heavy drops or dark silhouettes:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral canvas (`#F8FAFC`) with recessed toolbars and secondary panels (`#F1F5F9`) framed by subtle 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`), with a whisper-soft ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts):** Used when hovering generated modules, drop-down menus, and context actions: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05)`, border shifts subtly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Dialogs):** Curriculum selector modals, assessment builder overlays, and PDF export dialogs: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06)`, framed with a darkened backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background-color: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (Level 2). This standard creates a modern, accessible atmosphere while preserving the structural credibility required for official scholastic paperwork:

- Standard buttons, input controls, chips, and table wrappers utilize `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Cards, prompt panels, and module containers scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Modals, prominent generator action boards, and large template previews adopt `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Micro status tags, counter dots, and category avatars use full-radius pill styling for clear affordance separation against rectangular document structures.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curriculum Phase Badges (e.g., Fase D - Kelas 7-9):** Solid tint background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E3A8A`, border `1px solid #DBEAFE`, font `label-md`, radius `9999px`.
- **Status Badges:** Completed/Terverifikasi: `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#A7F3D0` border. Draft/Revisi: `#FFFBEB` background, `#92400E` text, `#FDE68A` border.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** 1px border `#E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Focus state: border `#2563EB`, ring `3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Subject / Topic Selector Card:** Selectable interactive surface with 1px border `#E2E8F0`, elevating with a secondary cobalt border (`#2563EB`) and soft fill (`#EFF6FF`) when activated.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `1.125rem` x `1.125rem`. Unchecked: border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: background `#2563EB`, border `#2563EB`, with white check/radio dot. Smooth transition `150ms ease-in-out`.

### Cards & Module Containers
- Resting surface: `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `1rem`, internal padding `1.5rem`.
- Features a header strip option containing subject metadata, lesson duration tag, and Kurikulum Merdeka compliance indicator.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Pancasila Profile Indicator (Dimensi Profil Pelajar Pancasila):** Horizontal pill cluster displaying attributes (e.g., *Bernalar Kritis*, *Gotong Royong*, *Kreatif*) using muted indigo backgrounds (`#EEF2F6`) and navy text (`#1E3A8A`).
- **Prompt Generator Stepper:** Guided 4-step wizard rail (1. Fase & Mapel -> 2. Capaian Pembelajaran -> 3. Kegiatan Pembelajaran -> 4. Asesmen) showing active step in `#2563EB` with numeric circle badges and connected progression line.
- **Export Toolbar:** Floating bottom-right action bar for instant download to Word (.docx), PDF, or Google Drive, styled in Level 2 elevation with contrasting dark navy container `#0F2942` and light text.
